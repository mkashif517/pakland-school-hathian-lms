Admission Guidelines

  • Submit the online form with correct details of the student and the parent / guardian.

  • Keep your Application Number safe. You need it to check your admission status.

  • Visit the school with the required documents and meet the school administration.

  • The student may be asked to take a short test or interview to find the right class.

  • Fees are paid at the school office once the admission is confirmed.

Admission FAQs

What documents are required for admission?

A copy of the B-Form, the parent or guardian's CNIC, the previous result card (if applicable) and photographs. Bring them when you visit the school.

How do I check my admission status?

Use the Application Number you received after submitting the form (for example PKL-2026-0001) in the Check Admission Status box above.

How much is the school fee?

Fees depend on the class. Please see the School Fee Structure above, or contact the school office on 0300-9307310.
