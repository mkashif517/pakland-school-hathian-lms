About Pakland — Common Questions

How can I apply for admission?

Open the Admissions Portal from the home page, complete the online form and save your Application Number. Follow the school's instructions for any next steps.

Which classes does the school offer?

Pakland School Hathian offers classes from Play Group to Class 10.

How can I check my admission status?

Enter your Application Number (for example PKL-2026-0001) in the Check Admission Status box in the Admissions section.

How do parents see diary, attendance and marks?

Use Student Login from the menu. The student portal shows the daily diary, attendance, marks and school notices.

Which documents are required for admission?

A copy of the B-Form, the father's CNIC, the previous school result and photographs.
